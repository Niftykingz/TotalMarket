What this page shows

This page lists the latest closing (or clearly marked intraday) level of the world's main stock indices, each with its date, time and source.

Why it matters

Overnight moves in the US and Asia often set the mood for Indian markets the next morning, because global funds move money across countries and the same worries (oil, US interest rates, AI stocks) affect everyone.

What to watch

  • The US close each evening (IST night) and GIFT Nifty before 9:15 am
  • US 10-year bond yields and the dollar
  • Big moves in Asian chip stocks such as Korea and Taiwan
