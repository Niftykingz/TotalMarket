About the Nifty 50

The Nifty 50 is NSE's main index of 50 large, actively traded Indian companies across sectors. Each company's weight depends on the value of its shares available for trading (free-float market capitalisation), so the biggest companies move the index most. NSE reviews the list twice a year, which is why companies are occasionally added or removed.

The official, current list of the 50 companies and their weights is published by NSE Indices on niftyindices.com. We link to it rather than copying it, because it changes after each review.
