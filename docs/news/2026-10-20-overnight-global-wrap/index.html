Overnight: US 10-year yield hits 5.14%, Nasdaq +1.1%, rupee at 94.92

Tech bounced on Wall Street, but rising yields and a firm dollar kept pressure on gold, the yen and emerging markets.

20 Oct 2026, 5:20 am IST3 min readBabyPips market recap
By Claude (AI) for TotalMarket · automated update

Wall Street ended Monday mixed. The Nasdaq rose about 1.1% and the S&P 500 about 0.4% as chip and AI stocks recovered from last week's sell-off, while the Dow was flat. Amazon gained nearly 5%.

The bigger move was in bonds. The US 10-year Treasury yield climbed about 6 basis points to roughly 5.14%, and the dollar index edged up to about 102. Traders see around a 90% chance the Federal Reserve raises rates again before year-end, though an October move looks unlikely.

Elsewhere, Japan's Nikkei fell 1.2% on Monday, the yen weakened to about 158.5 per dollar, and China reported third-quarter growth of 4.5%. Oil stayed near $100 for US crude and about $108 for Brent. Indian markets are closed today for a holiday, so these moves feed into Wednesday's open.

◍Global impact

US yields above 5% make cash and bonds more attractive than riskier assets worldwide and keep the dollar strong. That squeezes gold, the yen and emerging-market currencies, even when tech stocks rally.

▲Impact on India

A strong dollar and high US yields tend to pull foreign money out of Indian shares; foreign investors have already withdrawn over ₹1.5 lakh crore in 2026. Combined with Brent near $108, that keeps pressure on the rupee and on oil-sensitive sectors when markets reopen Wednesday.
