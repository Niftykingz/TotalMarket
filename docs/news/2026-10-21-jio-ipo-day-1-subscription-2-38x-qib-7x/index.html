Jio IPO day 1: 2.38x subscribed by 3 pm; QIBs bid 7x, retail at 0.61x

Bids for about 47 crore shares came in on the first day of India's biggest IPO.

21 Oct 2026, 3:54 pm IST2 min readHDFC Sky: Jio IPO day 1 at 3 pm
By Boss Piggy AI · TotalMarket AI market desk

Companies in this story

RELIANCEReliance IndustriesRELIANCE · All news →

Jio Platforms' IPO was subscribed 2.38 times by 3 pm on day one, HDFC Sky reported: bids came in for about 46.95 crore shares against 19.75 crore on offer to the public (excluding the anchor portion already sold on Monday).

Big institutions (QIBs) led with 7.09 times their quota. HNIs, the non-institutional investors who apply for more than ₹2 lakh, were at 1.06 times. Reliance shareholders (1.15x) and employees (1.17x) also filled their reserved portions. Retail investors, applying up to ₹2 lakh, were at 0.61 times, which is normal on day one; many retail applicants bid on the last day.

What does this mean for allotment? If the retail portion ends oversubscribed, each applicant usually gets at most one lot of 12 shares, chosen by lottery. Worked example: at 5 times retail subscription, roughly 1 in 5 applicants would get a lot, and the rest get their blocked money released.

Key takeaway: institutional demand is very strong; retail and HNI bids on 22–23 October will decide the final number before allotment on 26 October.

What happened

By 3 pm on day one, investors had bid for about 46.95 crore Jio shares against 19.75 crore on offer, 2.38 times. Institutions bid 7.09 times their quota; retail investors 0.61 times.

Why it matters

Institutional demand is strong. If the retail portion ends oversubscribed, shares are allotted by lottery, usually one lot per successful applicant.

What to watch next

  • Retail and HNI bids on 22–23 October
  • Final subscription figures after the issue closes on 23 October
  • Allotment on 26 October
