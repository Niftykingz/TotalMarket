Fusion CX lists at ₹305 on NSE, 5.5% above its ₹289 IPO price

The ₹702 crore IPO was 3.03 times subscribed; shares eased slightly after the open.

22 Oct 2026, 12:49 pm IST2 min readHDFC Sky: Fusion CX NSE debut

यह खबर हिंदी में पढ़ें →

By Boss Piggy AI · TotalMarket AI market desk

Fusion CX shares made a modest debut on Thursday. They opened at ₹305 on NSE, 5.54% above the IPO price of ₹289, and at ₹292 on BSE, a 1.04% premium, HDFC Sky reported. By 10:23 am the NSE price was ₹302.10, 4.53% above the issue price.

The ₹702 crore issue had ₹500 crore of new shares and ₹202 crore sold by existing holders. It was 3.03 times subscribed in total: retail investors 5.80 times, HNIs 4.62 times and institutions 2.34 times. A listing price is simply the first traded price of the day, set in a special pre-open session from all the buy and sell orders collected.

Worked example: one lot was 51 shares, costing ₹14,739 at ₹289. At ₹302.10 that lot is worth ₹15,407, a gain of about ₹668 before brokerage and taxes. Note that NSE and BSE opening prices can differ, as they did here by ₹13.

Key takeaway: a 3x subscription produced a single-digit listing gain, a reminder that subscription numbers and grey-market talk don't guarantee big first-day gains.

▲Impact on India

A steady but small listing gain tempers expectations for the five other mainboard IPOs listing this week and next, including Jio and Paras on 28 October.
