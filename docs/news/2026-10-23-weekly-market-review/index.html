What Moved

The Nifty 50 closed the week at 22,600.00, down 2.36% from the previous Friday's 23,146.15, and fell 0.39% on Friday itself. The damage was far worse below the top 50 stocks: the Nifty Next 50 lost 4.58% over the week, the Midcap 100 5.63% and the Smallcap 100 7.27%.

Breadth

On Friday 450 stocks in the Nifty 500 fell and 50 rose, according to NSE. India VIX, which measures the volatility traders expect, rose 6.32% to 16.76.

Sectors

Every sector index in the heatmap fell on Friday. Over the week consumer durables (−6.42%), metal (−5.40%), media (−5.15%) and realty (−5.04%) fell most; IT edged up 0.22%.

Flows

NSE's provisional data show foreign portfolio investors bought a net ₹887.65 crore of shares on Friday and domestic institutions a net ₹3,235.36 crore, even as prices fell.

Why It Mattered

Reuters linked the week's losses to surging oil prices amid Middle East tensions, which raise concerns about inflation, company margins and the rupee.

What to watch next week

  • Crude oil and the rupee
  • Second-quarter earnings from large companies
  • Whether institutional buying continues
  • India VIX, which rose 6.32% on Friday
▲Impact on India

Losses were concentrated in mid and small caps, so many portfolios fell more than the Nifty 50 suggests. Check breadth and sector moves, not just the headline indices.
