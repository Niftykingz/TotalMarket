Jio IPO day 2: 4.3x subscribed, retail portion fully booked; Paras Healthcare at 0.21x

Institutions bid 9.6 times their quota by midday; one day left for both IPOs.

22 Oct 2026, 12:48 pm ISTUpdated 22 Oct 2026, 3:55 pm IST2 min readBusiness Standard: Stock Market LIVE, 22 Oct

What changedUpdated to 4.3x total (QIB 9.78x, NII 4.09x, retail 1.39x) as of 2:12 pm, from 3.7x at 12:10 pm.

Jio Platforms' IPO was subscribed 4.3 times by 2:12 pm on its second day, up from 3.7 times at 12:10 pm, Business Standard reported: institutions (QIBs) 9.78 times, HNIs 4.09 times and retail investors 1.39 times. The retail portion crossing 1 means every share set aside for small investors has now been applied for.

Paras Healthcare's IPO was 0.21 times subscribed at 11:30 am, according to ScanX: retail 1.00 times, HNIs 0.20 times and institutions yet to bid. Institutions often bid on the last day, so the total can change sharply on Friday.

Worked example: with retail at 1.39 times, there are about 139 applications' worth of shares for every 100 available, so not every applicant will get a lot. Allotment is decided by lottery on 26 October.

Key takeaway: Jio's demand is broad across categories; Paras depends on institutions arriving on the final day.
