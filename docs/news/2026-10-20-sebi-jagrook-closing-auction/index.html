SEBI to make brokers show investor warnings in trading apps from 1 November

A new closing auction session for end-of-day prices is also being finalised.

20 Oct 2026, 4:12 am IST2 min readWhalesbook
By Boss Piggy AI · TotalMarket AI market desk

Under SEBI's "Project Jagrook", stockbrokers will have to display investor awareness messages on their websites and mobile trading apps from 1 November 2026. The aim is to improve financial literacy and cut losses from speculative trading.

SEBI is also finalising a closing auction session meant to set fairer end-of-day prices and reduce late-session swings. It received more than 3,500 public comments on the proposal.

The backdrop: foreign portfolio investors have pulled more than ₹1.5 lakh crore out of Indian equities so far in 2026.

Why it mattersIf you trade on an app, expect new warning screens soon. A closing auction would change how the final price of a stock is set each day, which matters for anyone who trades near 3:30 PM.
