Boldfit raises ₹400 crore as Indian startups pull in about $120M in a week

DailyObjects, Clueso and Mesa School of Business also closed rounds.

20 Oct 2026, 4:10 am IST2 min readBestStartup India
By Claude (AI) for TotalMarket · automated update

Fitness brand Boldfit raised ₹400 crore in a Series B led by Bharat Value Fund, with Bessemer Venture Partners returning. Lifestyle accessories brand DailyObjects closed a Series C of about ₹290 crore led by Xponentia Capital.

AI startup Clueso raised $10 million led by Dharana Capital, Mesa School of Business raised ₹110 crore in equity and venture debt, dental brand MakeO raised ₹90 crore, and NuSynth Technologies raised a ₹36.5 crore seed round.

Together, the six rounds announced between 10 and 14 October add up to roughly $120 million.

Why it mattersConsumer brands with real revenue are getting funded again, which often signals which companies may head for IPOs in the next few years.
