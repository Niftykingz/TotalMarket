Oil stays above $100 as Houthi attacks hit Saudi targets

Brent near $108 keeps pressure on India's import bill and the rupee.

20 Oct 2026, 4:14 am IST1 min readTheStreet
By Boss Piggy AI · TotalMarket AI market desk

Crude briefly eased on Monday after the US waived some sanctions on Russia, then climbed again as Houthi attacks on Saudi targets revived supply worries. US crude rose more than 2% to about $103.70, and Brent traded around $108.

For India, which imports most of its oil, sustained prices at this level raise the import bill and feed into inflation and pressure on the rupee.

▲Impact on India

India imports most of its crude, so every sustained rise lifts the import bill, inflation and pressure on the rupee. Oil marketing companies, airlines, paints and tyre makers are usually the most affected.

Why it mattersOil is the single biggest outside risk for Indian markets right now. Oil marketing companies, airlines, paints and tyres are usually the most sensitive sectors.
