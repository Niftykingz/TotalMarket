Benchmarks

On Wednesday, 28 October, the Nifty 50 closed at 22,698.85, down 50.90 points or 0.22%. The Sensex closed at 73,246.03, lower by 352.64 points or 0.48%. Nifty Bank gained 292.50 points or 0.54% to 54,267.40. The Nifty 50 recovered from its session low of 22,497.70, finishing above the intraday trough.

Breadth And Volatility

Nifty 50 constituents split 26 advances, 24 declines and 0 unchanged. Nifty Bank constituents were evenly split at 7 advances and 7 declines. Nifty Midcap 100 gained 0.43% and Nifty Smallcap 100 rose 0.90%. India VIX closed at 16.36, down 0.28%. Total-market advances/declines and cash turnover were not independently verified; this report shows index-constituent breadth only.

Sector Heatmap

Auto +1.45%, Pharma +1.04%, Financial Services +0.47% and Bank +0.54%. On the softer side, IT fell 1.98%, Realty 0.89%, Energy 0.89%, FMCG 0.56% and Metal 0.32%. These are index moves, not explanations by themselves.

Nifty 50 Movers

Gainers: Apollo Hospitals +4.43%, Larsen & Toubro +3.72%, Trent +3.31%, SBI Life Insurance +2.06% and InterGlobe Aviation +2.03%. Decliners: Infosys -3.43%, Reliance Industries -3.19%, ONGC -2.16%, Tech Mahindra -1.92% and Bharti Airtel -1.55%.

Institutional Flows

The latest available provisional cash-market row is 27 October, not 28 October. FII/FPI bought ₹24,517.65 crore and sold ₹20,277.26 crore, net +₹4,240.39 crore. DIIs bought ₹15,702.74 crore and sold ₹16,952.77 crore, net -₹1,250.03 crore. Month-to-date through 27 October remained net negative for FIIs and net positive for DIIs, as shown on the flow dashboard. These are provisional cash flows. The collector’s 28 October 19:28 IST fetch had no matching-date flow row; no same-day value is inferred.

Drivers

Reuters reported crude prices rose after the Houthi group claimed a missile strike targeting Saudi Arabia’s largest refinery; Saudi authorities had not immediately confirmed the attack. Higher oil renews concern about imported inflation and margins in India. Indian IT and energy shares weighed on benchmarks, while global technology stocks had rallied overnight. The contrast suggests local oil and sector-specific concerns mattered; no single cause is established.

Corporate Developments

Larsen & Toubro gained 3.72% after reporting Q2 FY27 net profit of ₹5,202 crore, up 32.5% year on year, according to Financial Express. Jio Platforms made a strong market debut at a premium to its issue price, but that strength did not offset pressure in heavyweight IT and energy names.

Global Context

US benchmarks finished higher on 27 October as technology shares advanced. Asian markets were broadly firmer on 28 October, led by a sharp rally in South Korea after SK Hynix results; Japan also gained. Investors were cautious ahead of the Federal Reserve’s policy decision later on 28 October.

Next Session

Factual Takeaway

Nifty and Sensex declined, but Bank Nifty, midcaps and smallcaps advanced. Auto and pharma were relative strengths; IT was the clearest selected-sector laggard. The latest confirmed FII/DII row remains 27 October.

  • Watch scheduled Q2 results from Coal India, Asian Paints, InterGlobe Aviation, Trent and Laurus Labs on the next session.
