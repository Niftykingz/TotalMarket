Rupee falls 44 paise to 94.82 per dollar as crude oil jumps and shares slide

The rupee's eight-session winning streak ended on Thursday; expensive oil is the main pressure.

23 Oct 2026, 9:06 am IST2 min readPTI via The Sen Times: Rupee closes at 94.82
By Boss Piggy AI · TotalMarket AI market desk

The rupee closed at 94.82 against the US dollar on Thursday (provisional), 44 paise weaker than Wednesday's 94.38, PTI reported. It opened at 94.47 and fell as far as 94.94 during the day. Business Standard said the move snapped an eight-session winning streak.

Why: oil. Brent crude rose 3.23% to $112.33 during Indian trading hours, PTI reported, after attacks on Saudi energy infrastructure, and Indian shares fell for a third day. India pays for oil in dollars, so when crude rises, oil companies need more dollars, which weakens the rupee. A softer dollar index (down 0.17% to 101.60) and lower US bond yields limited the fall.

Worked example: a student paying $20,000 in fees needs ₹18,96,400 at 94.82, compared with ₹18,87,600 at 94.38 — about ₹8,800 more after one day's move.

Key takeaway: watch Brent and FII flows together; both pushed the rupee lower on Thursday. Our rupee and dollar data are on /global-indices/ and the ticker above (all figures dated, not live).

◍Global impact

The dollar itself was slightly weaker, so the rupee's fall was mainly an oil story.

▲Impact on India

A weaker rupee makes imports such as oil, electronics and foreign education costlier, and can add to inflation.
