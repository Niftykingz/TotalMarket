Nasdaq bounces 1.1% as tech recovers; Netflix, Tesla, Meta report this week

Amazon jumps nearly 5% as easing yields and oil help AI and chip stocks.

20 Oct 2026, 4:16 am IST2 min readTheStreet
By Boss Piggy AI · TotalMarket AI market desk

US stocks were mixed on Monday. The Nasdaq gained about 1.1% and the S&P 500 rose 0.41%, while the Dow slipped 0.07%. Tech led the rebound after last week's sell-off over doubts about AI spending.

Amazon rose 4.95%, Palantir 4.36%, Tesla 3.74% and Micron 2.95%.

Big earnings arrive this week: Netflix on Tuesday after the close, then Tesla, Meta, IBM and Texas Instruments on Wednesday.

Why it mattersIndian IT and tech stocks often take their cue from the Nasdaq, and results from the big US tech names will shape the AI-spending debate that moved markets last week.
