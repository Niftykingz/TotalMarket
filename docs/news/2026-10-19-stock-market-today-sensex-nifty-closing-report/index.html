Stock market today: Sensex sinks 876 pts, Nifty 22,889; PSU banks only gainers

Private banks and IT led a broad sell-off; midcaps and smallcaps fell over 1% and investors lost about ₹5.5 lakh crore.

20 Oct 2026, 5:45 am IST4 min read5paisa post-market
By Claude (AI) for TotalMarket · automated update

Indian shares fell sharply on Monday, ending a two-day winning streak. The Sensex dropped 876.37 points (1.17%) to 73,852.90 and the Nifty 50 lost 257.20 points (1.11%) to 22,888.95 after opening near 23,150 and touching a high of about 23,175. Profit-taking after last week's strong rally, weaker results from private lenders, Brent crude above $108 and a weekly-expiry swing all weighed.

The fall was broad: about three stocks fell for every one that rose on the NSE, smallcaps did worst, and the BSE's total market value shrank by roughly ₹5.54 lakh crore. Public-sector banks were the one bright spot, with the PSU Bank index up 2.46% on strong quarterly numbers from Union Bank and PNB.

Indian markets are shut on Tuesday for a holiday. When trading resumes on Wednesday, the Jio Platforms IPO opens and big results start to arrive.

Key takeaways

  • Nifty fell 1.11% and snapped a two-day rally; it closed near the day's low after opening around 23,150.
  • Private banks dragged the most: Axis Bank fell 4.19% after its September-quarter profit missed estimates.
  • PSU banks were the only major sector in the green (+2.46%), led by Union Bank (+5.3%) and PNB (+4.8%).
  • Smallcaps (−1.29%) fell more than the Nifty, and declining stocks outnumbered gainers about 3 to 1.
  • India VIX rose about 5% to near 14.7, a sign traders are paying more for protection.
