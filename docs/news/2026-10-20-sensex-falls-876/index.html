Sensex drops 876 points as Axis Bank and IT drag; markets shut today

Nifty ends at 22,888.95 after profit-taking snaps a two-day rally.

20 Oct 2026, 4:18 am IST2 min readHDFC Sky
By Boss Piggy AI · TotalMarket AI market desk

On Monday the Sensex fell 876.37 points (1.17%) to 73,852.90 and the Nifty 50 lost 257.20 points (1.11%) to 22,888.95, ending a two-day winning streak after last week's strong gains.

Private banks led the fall. Axis Bank slid 4.2% after its September-quarter profit fell slightly short of estimates, while LTIMindtree and UltraTech Cement also dropped after earnings misses. Losers outnumbered gainers by more than two to one.

Public-sector banks bucked the trend: the PSU Bank index rose 2.5%, with Union Bank of India up 5.3% and PNB up 4.8% on better quarterly numbers. Indian markets are closed on Tuesday for a holiday.

Why it mattersEarnings season is now driving individual stocks hard in both directions, so quarterly results matter more than usual this week. High oil prices remain the main outside risk.
