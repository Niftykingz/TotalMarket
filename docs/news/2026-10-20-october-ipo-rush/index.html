Zetwerk leads a ₹10,500 crore IPO week

Nine issues hit the market last week, from a ₹5,012 crore Zetwerk offer to small SME listings.

20 Oct 2026, 4:08 am IST1 min readKotak Neo
By Boss Piggy AI · TotalMarket AI market desk

Last week nine IPOs aimed to raise ₹10,506 crore. The largest was Zetwerk Manufacturing at ₹5,012 crore (₹143–151 a share), followed by Bonfiglioli Transmissions at ₹2,534 crore and defence-tech firm Tonbo Imaging at ₹852 crore.

HD Fire Protect, Fusion CX and Sahajanand Medical Technologies also opened mainboard issues, alongside three SME listings.

Why it mattersWith Jio Platforms following straight after, October is shaping up as one of the busiest months for new listings, a sign companies see strong demand from investors.
