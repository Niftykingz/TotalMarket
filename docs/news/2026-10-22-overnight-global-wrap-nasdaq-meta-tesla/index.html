Overnight: Nasdaq +1.05% on AI rally; Meta up, Tesla down after hours; GIFT Nifty signals weak start

Wall Street rose for a fourth day, but GIFT Nifty points lower as oil stays above $108 and Indian broader markets stay fragile.

22 Oct 2026, 8:50 am IST3 min readTradingKey: US market recap 21 Oct

US stocks rose again on Wednesday. The Dow gained 0.69% to 52,856.09, the S&P 500 added 0.58% to 7,757.17 and the tech-heavy Nasdaq rose 1.05% to 26,702.86, helped by strong results from SpaceX and server maker Supermicro, which lifted AI-linked shares, TradingKey reported. Netflix fell about 5% on a weak outlook.

After the closing bell, the two big results came in. Meta's shares rose about 2–3% in after-hours trade after revenue of $64.6 billion beat expectations, while Tesla's fell about 3.3% after profit dropped 43%. After-hours trading is thin, so these moves can change when markets open. In Asia, South Korea's Kospi jumped 3.29% on Wednesday after Tuesday's crash.

Oil swung but ended flat. Brent briefly rose about $2 to $110.85 when Iran's leaders ruled out talks under pressure, then settled at $108.82, up just 3 cents; US crude (WTI) settled at $97.15. Gold futures edged up 0.2% to about $4,150. GIFT Nifty, the Nifty futures contract traded in Gujarat's GIFT City that hints at India's opening, was at 22,644 at 7:35 am, down 0.37% and below Wednesday's Nifty close of 22,811.35.

Key takeaway: a strong US session isn't enough on its own; GIFT Nifty points to a soft open in India, where oil above $108 and weak mid and small caps remain the worry.
