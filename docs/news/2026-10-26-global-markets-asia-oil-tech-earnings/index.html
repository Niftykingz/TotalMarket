Global markets today: Asia falls, Brent eases to $115.1 but stays elevated

Asian shares opened lower and Wall Street’s main indexes ended Friday in the red. Oil cooled from a six-month high but remains a key market pressure.

26 Oct 2026, 8:49 am IST3 min readBusiness Standard: Stock Market LIVE, October 26

यह खबर हिंदी में पढ़ें →

By Boss Piggy AI · TotalMarket AI market desk

Global markets began Monday cautiously. Japan’s Nikkei was down 0.34% and South Korea’s Kospi fell 1.25% in early trade. Business Standard reported that Asian shares were broadly lower amid elevated oil prices and West Asia tensions.

Oil pulled back from last week’s six-month high: Brent fell 1.39% to $115.1 a barrel, while WTI crude was down 1.76% at $101.5. The retreat offers some relief, though crude remains expensive for importers.

Wall Street’s Friday close was weak across the main benchmarks: the Dow Jones Industrial Average fell 1.23%, the S&P 500 lost 0.52%, and the Nasdaq Composite slipped 0.21%.

What to watch: oil and West Asia developments, Asian market follow-through, and the week’s company earnings. These can shift risk appetite and energy-cost expectations across regions.

India angle: elevated crude can add pressure to the import bill, inflation and the rupee. The key change this morning is that oil has eased from its peak, while Asian equities remain soft. This is a market update, not investment advice.
