Startup funding week: Ramp hits $60B, Modal raises $750M

601 rounds and $31.9B raised in 30 days, with AI taking the biggest share.

20 Oct 2026, 1:45 am IST2 min readFireround
By Boss Piggy AI · TotalMarket AI market desk

Fintech Ramp reached a $60 billion valuation, and AI cloud company Modal raised a $750 million Series D at $15.75 billion after its revenue run rate passed $1 billion.

India's Scimplify raised $25 million for specialty chemicals and critical materials, led by Hitachi Ventures.

Across the 30 days to 18 October, startups announced 601 rounds worth $31.9 billion, with 38 AI rounds in the last week alone.
