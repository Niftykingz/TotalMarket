Gold rate today: 24K falls to about ₹1,46,070 per 10 g, silver near ₹2.3 lakh/kg

Shop rates eased overnight as a firmer rupee made imported gold cheaper; global prices were steady.

22 Oct 2026, 8:48 am IST2 min readAsianet Newsable: gold, silver prices 22 Oct

यह खबर हिंदी में पढ़ें →

By Boss Piggy AI · TotalMarket AI market desk

Indian gold prices are lower this morning. 24-carat gold is quoted at ₹14,607 per gram (about ₹1,46,070 per 10 grams) in Mumbai, Chennai, Kolkata, Bengaluru and Hyderabad, and ₹14,622 in Delhi, according to Asianet Newsable's 7:35 am update. 22-carat, the usual jewellery grade, is ₹13,389 per gram (₹1,33,890 per 10 g). Silver is ₹2,29,900 per kg.

Why lower? Mainly the rupee. It strengthened about 0.5% on Wednesday to roughly 94.43–94.46 per dollar, and because India imports almost all its gold, a stronger rupee makes each gram cheaper even when global prices barely move. Global gold futures were steady at about $4,150 an ounce. Compared with Asianet's rates yesterday, 24K in Mumbai is about ₹2,256 per 10 g lower.

Worked example: a 10 g, 22K chain at ₹1,33,890 for the metal, plus a 12% making charge (₹16,067) and 3% GST on both (₹4,499), comes to about ₹1,54,456. The making charge is the part you can negotiate. The official IBJA benchmark for today is published around midday; the latest one we have verified is from 19 October.

Key takeaway: when the rupee strengthens, Indian gold can get cheaper even if world prices don't move.
