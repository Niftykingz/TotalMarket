Overnight: Dow jumps 530 pts, oil eases off $110, GIFT Nifty points to firm start

A rotation into healthcare and value stocks lifted the Dow to a record-style rally while tech stood still; lower oil and yields calmed nerves.

21 Oct 2026, 8:50 am IST3 min readHDFC Sky: US market close
By Claude (AI) for TotalMarket · automated update

Wall Street had a good night for 'old economy' shares. The Dow Jones rose 529.85 points (1.02%) to 52,495.88 and the S&P 500 gained 0.28% to 7,712.57, but the tech-heavy Nasdaq was flat at 26,424.82 (-0.02%). Small companies did best: the Russell 2000 rose 1.65%.

Why the split? Investors moved money out of big tech names like Apple and Meta and into healthcare, utilities and financials. This is called sector rotation: the same pool of money shifting from one group of stocks to another. Healthcare jumped about 2.3% to a record, helped by Eli Lilly, Merck and UnitedHealth, and 3M rose 5.8% on strong results. General Motors fell 6.8% even after beating estimates, because it narrowed its yearly outlook and warned of higher raw-material costs.

Oil and bonds also helped. Brent crude briefly topped $110 a barrel, a five-month high, before settling at $108.79, and the US 10-year Treasury yield, the interest rate on 10-year US government bonds, slipped to about 5.09–5.12%. Lower yields make shares look more attractive versus safe bonds. In Asia, South Korea's Kospi crashed 3.73% on Tuesday on AI-chip worries, but it was rebounding about 4% early on Wednesday, according to Reuters. GIFT Nifty, a Nifty futures contract traded in Gujarat's GIFT City that hints at how Indian markets may open, was at 23,012.50 at 7:35 am, about 120 points above Nifty's last close of 22,888.95.

Key takeaway: global markets are calmer this morning than on Monday, but oil near $110 and US yields above 5% remain the two numbers that can quickly turn sentiment.
