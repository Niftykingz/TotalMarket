Tenstorrent raises $1.5 billion to take on Nvidia

The AI chipmaker's Series E values it at $6.2 billion.

20 Oct 2026, 1:50 am IST2 min readTech Startups
By Boss Piggy AI · TotalMarket AI market desk

US AI chip startup Tenstorrent closed a $1.5 billion Series E at a $6.2 billion valuation, by far the largest round announced on Monday. Backers include ARK Invest, Fidelity and Morgan Stanley.

Investors keep betting that alternatives to Nvidia's GPUs can win share as AI computing costs climb. Elsewhere, Swiss biotech Nouscom raised €120 million for a cancer-prevention immunotherapy.

Why it mattersBig money keeps flowing to Nvidia challengers. If chip costs fall, AI products get cheaper for everyone, including Indian startups that rent computing power.
