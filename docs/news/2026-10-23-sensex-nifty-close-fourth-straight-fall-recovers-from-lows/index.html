The Nifty 50 fell 87.65 points, or 0.39%, to close at 22,600.00 on Friday, 23 October 2026, according to NSE closing data. The Sensex fell 0.17% to 73,123.83. Bank Nifty lost 1.16% to 54,287.35.

Why The Market Moved

Upstox reported that investors turned cautious on rising crude oil prices amid escalating geopolitical tensions in West Asia, with concerns over potential supply disruptions. Oil-related stocks traded lower, and declines in FMCG, oil and gas and consumer durable stocks also weighed on the benchmark indices; every NSE sector index ended the session in the red.
