MCX gold slips to ₹1,45,972, silver falls 1% as Brent climbs to $110

Higher oil revives inflation worries and keeps US rate-hike fears alive, pressuring precious metals.

21 Oct 2026, 12:47 pm IST2 min readAnalytics Insight: MCX gold, Brent
By Boss Piggy AI · TotalMarket AI market desk

Gold futures on MCX, India's commodity exchange, fell 0.37% to ₹1,45,972 per 10 grams for December delivery, and silver futures fell 1.08% to ₹2,09,207 per kg, Analytics Insight reported this morning. Global spot gold was down 0.2% at $4,118 an ounce and spot silver 0.9% lower at $57.44.

Why does oil matter for gold? Brent crude rose 1.47% to about $110.4 a barrel. Expensive oil pushes up inflation, and high inflation makes the US Federal Reserve more likely to keep interest rates high or raise them at its 27–28 October meeting. Higher rates make gold, which pays no interest, less attractive than bonds or deposits.

Futures prices differ from shop prices. MCX is where traders buy and sell gold for future delivery; jewellers' rates add GST and local margins, which is why this morning's shop rates for 24K were higher, at about ₹1.47–1.49 lakh per 10 g.

Key takeaway: for now oil is steering gold; a fall in crude or softer Fed talk would ease the pressure.
