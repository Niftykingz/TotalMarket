Evening markets: Nasdaq slides about 2% on AI jitters; Brent above $111 after Aramco attack; gold slips

Meta fell about 5% despite beating forecasts, Tesla cut its 2026 spending plan, and European shares opened lower.

US stocks fell on Thursday. By about 11:20 am New York time (8:50 pm IST), the Nasdaq was down 2.07% at 26,149.19, the S&P 500 down 1.16% at 7,667.23 and the Dow down 0.22% at 52,737.84, Yahoo Finance's live page showed. Chip stocks led the fall on worries that spending on AI data centres could slow; Lam Research dropped about 9%.

Big Tech results moved shares. Meta was down about 5.6% even after beating revenue forecasts and announcing a 2-for-1 stock split, as investors weighed its rising AI spending. Tesla cut its 2026 capital spending plan to $20 billion from $25 billion, Fortune reported, raising questions about how fast it can move into AI and robots.

Oil kept climbing after a deadly attack on Saudi Arabia's Dammam airport and an Aramco oil field. Brent traded at $111.45 (+2.42%) and US crude at $98.53. Gold fell 1.9% to about $4,071 an ounce, and the US 10-year yield eased to about 5.01%. In Europe, the Stoxx 600 had fallen 0.92% in early trade.

Key takeaway: falling US tech shares and oil above $110 point to another cautious start for Indian markets on Friday; watch GIFT Nifty in the morning brief.
