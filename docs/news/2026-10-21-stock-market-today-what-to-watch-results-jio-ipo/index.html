Indian markets reopen today after Tuesday's Dussehra holiday. On Monday the Sensex fell 876 points (1.17%) to 73,852.90 and the Nifty 50 fell 257 points (1.11%) to 22,888.95. GIFT Nifty at 23,012.50 this morning suggests a firmer start, helped by the Dow's 530-point rally.

The big event is the Jio Platforms IPO, which opens today and closes on Friday. An IPO (initial public offering) is when a company sells shares to the public for the first time. The price band is ₹1,065–1,119, one lot is 12 shares (₹13,428), and the ₹30,213 crore issue is India's largest. Its anchor book, the portion sold to big institutions a day early, drew bids of over ₹2.5 lakh crore. Watch the day-wise subscription figures, which show how many times the shares on offer have been applied for.

Quarterly results due today include food-delivery company Eternal (which owns Zomato and Blinkit), Adani Green Energy and Mahindra & Mahindra Financial Services. On Thursday, HPCL, Havells, Swiggy and Colgate report. For the Nifty, Kotak Neo's analysts see support near 22,800 and resistance at 23,000–23,200. Support is a level where buyers have tended to step in; resistance is where sellers have tended to appear.

Key takeaway: today's mood will depend on whether foreign funds keep selling, how oil behaves near $110 and the early demand for Jio's IPO.

▲Impact on India

The Jio IPO will set a valuation marker for Reliance and telecom stocks, while results from Eternal and Adani Green will guide sentiment in new-age tech and renewable energy shares.

Why it mattersA huge IPO absorbs cash: investors may sell other shares to apply, which can weigh on the wider market during the subscription days.
