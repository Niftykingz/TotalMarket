Stock market today: Sensex sheds 460 pts, Nifty 22,687.65 as oil jumps to $112; media, FMCG, IT drag

A third straight fall as Brent jumped about 3% after attacks on Saudi Aramco; two shares fell for every one that rose, and FIIs sold about ₹3,459 crore.

22 Oct 2026, 8:40 pm IST4 min readIIFL: Sensex falls 460 points, Nifty below 22,700

Indian shares fell for a third straight session on Thursday. The Sensex closed 460.30 points (0.62%) lower at 73,249.39 and the Nifty 50 fell 123.70 points (0.54%) to 22,687.65, IIFL and HDFC Sky reported. Bank Nifty held up better, slipping 0.26% to 54,832.35, but the Nifty Midcap 100 fell 0.87% and the Smallcap 100 1.17%.

Oil was the trigger. Brent jumped about 3% to roughly $112.5 a barrel by 3:45 pm after attacks on Saudi Aramco's oilfields, Business Standard reported. India imports most of its crude, so costlier oil raises inflation worries and hurts companies that use fuel. Media (−2.34%), FMCG (−1.74%) and IT (−1.31%) were the weakest sectors; Nestle India fell after a food-safety regulator notice and eMudhra hit its 20% lower circuit again.

Foreign investors sold a net ₹3,458.70 crore on Thursday while domestic institutions bought ₹2,891.32 crore, according to Whalesbook; gross provisional figures were not yet available from the exchanges. On Wednesday FIIs had been small net buyers of ₹1,022.3 crore.

After the bell, results came in: Havells' profit rose 9.7% to ₹349.22 crore on revenue up 34.6%; Colgate-Palmolive India earned ₹341.6 crore (+4.3%); HPCL posted a consolidated profit of ₹3,625.22 crore, down 6.1% from a year ago; and Swiggy's loss narrowed to ₹922 crore as revenue rose 36%.

Key takeaway: with oil above $110 and foreign selling back, the market stays nervous; Friday's Infosys results and the end of the Jio IPO are the next big events.
