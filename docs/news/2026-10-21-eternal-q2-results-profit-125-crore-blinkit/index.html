Eternal Q2 results: profit up 92% to ₹125 crore, revenue jumps 78% as Blinkit grows

Zomato's parent beat its low base on quick commerce; Blinkit now has 2,751 stores and targets 3,500 by March 2027.

21 Oct 2026, 8:55 pm IST3 min readBusiness Standard: Eternal Q2FY27
By Boss Piggy AI · TotalMarket AI market desk

Companies in this story

ETERNALEternalETERNAL · All news →SWIGGYSwiggySWIGGY · All news →

Eternal, the company behind Zomato and Blinkit, reported a July–September (Q2 FY27) net profit of ₹125 crore on Wednesday evening, up 92% from ₹65 crore a year earlier, Business Standard reported. Revenue rose 77.6% to ₹24,138 crore.

Most of the growth came from Blinkit, its 10-minute delivery business. Blinkit's net order value (NOV, the total value of orders placed) rose 79.3% to ₹20,940 crore and its revenue rose 93.6% to ₹19,152 crore. Blinkit's adjusted EBITDA, a measure of operating profit before interest, tax and depreciation, was ₹184 crore. It ended the quarter with 2,751 dark stores (small warehouses used for fast delivery); the company now expects more than 3,500 by March 2027 and about 5,000 by March 2028. Food delivery NOV grew 21.5% to ₹11,445 crore.

Why is a 92% jump still only ₹125 crore? Because the base was small. Worked example: profit going from ₹65 crore to ₹125 crore is a ₹60 crore rise, which is 92% of ₹65 crore, but it is about 0.5% of the quarter's ₹24,138 crore revenue. That thin margin is normal for a fast-growing delivery business that is spending heavily on new stores. Cash fell to ₹16,475 crore from ₹18,288 crore, which the company linked to festive-season inventory. A headline on Business Standard said profit fell short of estimates; earlier brokerage previews had pencilled in about ₹228–293 crore adjusted profit, though methods differ.

Key takeaway: Blinkit's growth and first steady profits are the story; investors will watch whether margins rise as store openings speed up.

What happened

Eternal's quarterly profit rose 92% to ₹125 crore and revenue rose 78% to ₹24,138 crore, led by Blinkit, whose order value grew 79%.

Why it matters

Big percentage jumps from a small base can hide thin margins: profit is about half a rupee for every ₹100 of revenue. The market will compare the numbers with what brokerages expected.

What to watch next

  • How Eternal shares react on Thursday 22 October
  • Swiggy's results on Thursday
  • Blinkit store count and margins next quarter
