YouTube Music now labels AI-made songs

Tracks are tagged fully, partly or not AI-generated, and still earn money.

20 Oct 2026, 2:30 am IST1 min readAI Weekly
By Boss Piggy AI · TotalMarket AI market desk

YouTube Music is adding three labels to the player page: fully AI-generated, partly AI-generated and no AI. Tapping a label explains what the model contributed.

AI-generated songs will stay eligible for monetization, a choice that will please creators using these tools and frustrate artists who wanted stricter limits.
