IPO daily: Jio Platforms debuts at ₹1,358, 21.35% above issue price

Jio's listing price of ₹1,358 compared with its ₹1,119 offer. The ₹30,213 crore issue closed at 22.19× subscription.

28 Oct 2026, 5:38 pm IST4 min readTimes of India: Jio listing price and final subscription summary
By Boss Piggy AI · TotalMarket AI market desk

Jio Platforms began exchange trading on Wednesday at ₹1,358 on the NSE and BSE, around 21.35% above its ₹1,119 IPO issue price, Times of India reported. This is the debut/listing price, not a first-session closing quote. The closing quote is withheld in this edition because a corroborated exchange-linked close could not be recovered by the publication workflow.

The public offer was ₹30,213 crore and comprised 27 crore fresh equity shares, with no offer-for-sale component. The company said it planned to use up to ₹27,500 crore of net proceeds to repay or prepay borrowings of its subsidiary, Reliance Jio Infocomm, according to The Economic Times.

Before listing, the final subscription book was 22.19× overall. The QIB portion was subscribed 70.55×, NII 14× and retail 2.72×. Employee demand was 2.93× and the Reliance shareholder category 7.6×. These are final bidding figures reported for the 23 October close, not live post-listing demand. Retail applicants bid for a minimum of 12 shares, costing ₹13,428 at the upper price band.

IPO pipeline | EAAA India Alternatives was scheduled to conclude bidding on 28 October. BLS Polymers, SFC Environmental Technologies and Wiscon Industries are scheduled to close on 29 October. M.K. Sons Fine Jewels, Carver Roboweld and Ajwa Projects opened on 28 October and are scheduled to close on 30 October. Bombay Coated and Special Steels is scheduled to open on 29 October and close on 2 November.

Closed / allotment watch | Core4 Engineers closed its SME issue on 27 October; allotment was scheduled for 28 October and listing for 30 October. Jio and Paras Healthcare were both on the 28 October listing calendar. Jio's debut data is shown above; a verified Paras first-session price is not included here.

Subscription-data caution | Live subscription snapshots for some open offers differed materially across providers during this check, particularly for BLS Polymers. The current investor-category breakdowns could not be reconciled against a complete exchange-certified bid sheet. This report therefore shows Jio's final, historical category chart and does not label interim third-party values as definitive.

The TotalMarket IPO center links issue terms, price bands, lot sizes, issue sizes and official exchange / registrar pages. Check the issuer's latest offer document and the exchange's final subscription or allotment record. GMP is unofficial and excluded from this report.
