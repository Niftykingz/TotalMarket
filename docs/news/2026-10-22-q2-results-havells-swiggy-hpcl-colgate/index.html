Q2 results: Havells profit up 9.7%, Swiggy loss narrows to ₹922 crore, HPCL earns ₹3,625 crore

Colgate's profit rose 4.3%; HPCL's profit fell 6.1% from a year ago but it swung back from a large first-quarter loss.

Four big companies reported July–September results after Thursday's close. Havells India's consolidated profit rose 9.72% to ₹349.22 crore and revenue from operations jumped 34.57% to ₹6,432 crore, Business Standard reported; cables grew 47.9% to ₹2,999 crore on higher copper prices, and Lloyd consumer durables 28.4% to ₹615 crore.

Colgate-Palmolive India's profit rose 4.3% to ₹341.6 crore on revenue up 12.3% to ₹1,705.8 crore, with high-single-digit toothpaste volume growth. Swiggy's consolidated loss narrowed to ₹922 crore from ₹1,092 crore a year earlier as revenue grew 36.1% to ₹7,571 crore; its quick-commerce arm Instamart lost ₹895 crore, Business Today reported.

HPCL reported a consolidated profit of ₹3,625.22 crore (standalone ₹3,482.62 crore), 6.1% lower than a year ago but a sharp turnaround from a ₹12,264.67 crore loss in the April–June quarter, the Free Press Journal reported. Revenue rose 26% to ₹1,39,113.50 crore. 'Consolidated' includes subsidiaries and joint ventures; 'standalone' is the company alone, which is why there are two profit figures.

These figures are as reported by news outlets citing the companies' filings; the exchange filings were not yet available to us and figures will be checked against them. Adani Energy Solutions' numbers could not be confirmed tonight.

Key takeaway: Havells and Colgate grew steadily, Swiggy is losing less money, and HPCL's swing shows how much oil-marketing profits depend on crude prices and government LPG compensation.

What happened

Havells' profit rose 9.7%, Colgate's 4.3%, Swiggy's loss shrank to ₹922 crore and HPCL earned ₹3,625 crore in July–September.

Why it matters

Always check whether a profit figure is consolidated (with subsidiaries) or standalone. HPCL's two numbers differ by about ₹143 crore for that reason.
