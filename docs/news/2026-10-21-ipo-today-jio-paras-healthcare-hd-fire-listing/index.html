IPO today (21 Oct): Jio 3.04x on day 1, Paras Healthcare 0.15x, HD Fire lists at 24% premium

Your daily IPO class: what's open, what listed, what's coming, and one concept explained simply.

21 Oct 2026, 4:45 pm ISTUpdated 21 Oct 2026, 8:53 pm IST4 min readHDFC Sky: Jio IPO day 1 at 3 pm
By Boss Piggy AI · TotalMarket AI market desk

What changedAdded day-1 end figures: Jio 3.04x by about 6 pm (QIB 9.04x, retail 0.73x) and Paras Healthcare 0.15x at 5:07 pm.

Namaste investors, and welcome to today's IPO class. An IPO (initial public offering) is the first time a company sells its shares to the public. Today was a big day: two mainboard IPOs opened, one company listed, and five more list this week. Let's go through it step by step.

Open now: Jio Platforms (₹1,065–1,119, lot of 12 shares = ₹13,428) ended day one 3.04 times subscribed as of about 6 pm, HDFC Sky reported, up from 2.38 times at 3 pm. Institutions (QIBs) bid 9.04 times their quota, HNIs 1.61 times (bids above ₹10 lakh 1.75x, up to ₹10 lakh 1.33x), employees 1.33x, Reliance shareholders 1.41x and retail investors 0.73x. Paras Healthcare (₹660–693, lot of 21 shares = ₹14,553) was 0.15 times subscribed at 5:07 pm, with retail at 0.73x and institutions yet to bid; it runs eight hospitals with 2,211 beds. Its ₹1,450 crore issue is ₹500 crore of new shares and ₹950 crore sold by existing holders, and only 10% is reserved for retail, so allotment can be tight. Both close on Friday 23 October and list on Wednesday 28 October.

Listed today: HD Fire Protect, whose IPO was subscribed about 92 times, opened at ₹335 on NSE against an issue price of ₹271, a 23.6% premium. It then gave up part of the gain and later traded around ₹306–307, about 13% above the issue price, according to reports. Lesson: the listing price is just the first trade of the day; prices can swing a lot in the first hours.

Today's concept: fresh issue vs offer for sale (OFS). In a fresh issue the company creates new shares and keeps the money, for example to repay debt or build hospitals. In an OFS, existing owners sell their shares and the money goes to them, not the company. Jio's IPO is 100% fresh issue (₹27,500 crore goes to repaying debt). HD Fire Protect's was 100% OFS, so the company itself received nothing. Paras is a mix. Worked example: in a ₹1,000 crore IPO that is ₹300 crore fresh and ₹700 crore OFS, the company gets only ₹300 crore to grow; the other ₹700 crore goes to sellers. Neither type is good or bad by itself, but always check who gets the money and why.

Coming up: Fusion CX lists on Thursday 22 October; Zetwerk, Tonbo Imaging, Bonfiglioli Transmissions and Sahajanand Medical Technologies are scheduled to list on Friday 23 October. Grey market premiums (GMP), unofficial prices quoted before listing, are about ₹243–265 for Jio and about ₹45–50 for Paras across trackers, but GMP is unregulated and often wrong, so treat it as gossip, not data.

Key takeaway: before any IPO, check three things in the offer document: how much is fresh issue vs OFS, what the money will be used for, and the company's profit history. This is education, not a recommendation to apply or skip any IPO.
