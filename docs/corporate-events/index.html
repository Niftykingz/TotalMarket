What each event means

What is a dividend?

A dividend is a share of the company's profit paid to shareholders. Since India moved to T+1 settlement, the ex-date and record date are usually the same day, so you must already own the shares before that day to be eligible.

What is a financial results?

Quarterly results show the company's revenue and profit for the last three months. Share prices often move sharply on results day.

What is a results date?

The day the company's board meets to approve and announce its quarterly results. Companies must tell the exchange this date in advance.
