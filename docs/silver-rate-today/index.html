Class in 2 minutes: how to read a silver rate

  1. Know the purity. Silver 999 is 99.9% pure and is used for bars and coins. Jewellery and utensils are often 925 (sterling silver, 92.5% pure), so they are priced lower per gram before making charges.
  2. Know the unit. Silver is quoted per kilogram. Divide by 1,000 for one gram, or by 100 for 10 grams.
  3. Know what is included. The IBJA benchmark excludes 3% GST and making charges, so shops and coin sellers will charge more.
  4. Know why it moves. Silver follows global prices in dollars, the rupee's value and industrial demand from solar panels and electronics, so it often swings more than gold.

Questions people ask

What is the silver rate today per kg?

The IBJA benchmark for silver 999 on Mon 19 Oct 2026 (PM) is ₹2,08,750 per kilogram, before 3% GST.

What is the silver price per gram today?

At the Mon 19 Oct 2026 benchmark, silver 999 works out to ₹208.75 per gram, or ₹2,088 per 10 grams, before GST.

Why is the shop price of silver higher?

Shops add 3% GST and their own premium or making charges; city rates also include local costs and margins.

What is the difference between MCX silver and the shop rate?

MCX silver is a futures price for traders, for delivery on a future date. Shop rates are for physical silver today and include taxes and margins.
