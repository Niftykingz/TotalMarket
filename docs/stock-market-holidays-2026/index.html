NSE market timings

  • Pre-open session: 09:00 to 09:15 am IST, when orders are collected to set the opening price.
  • Normal market: 09:15 am to 3:30 pm IST.
  • Closing session: 3:30 to 4:00 pm IST, used to set the official closing price.

Holidays that fall on a weekend

  • 15 Feb (Sunday): Maha Shivaratri
  • 21 Mar (Saturday): Id-Ul-Fitr
  • 15 Aug (Saturday): Independence Day
  • 08 Nov (Sunday): Diwali Laxmi Pujan

Special sessions

  • 08 Nov 2026, Muhurat trading (Diwali). Timings to be notified by NSE in a separate circular.

What this page shows

This is the list of weekdays in 2026 when NSE's equity market is shut, plus the normal trading hours.

Why it matters

On a holiday you can't buy or sell shares, and money and shares don't settle, so a sale on the day before a holiday reaches your account a day later. SIP dates that fall on a holiday usually move to the next working day.

What to watch

  • NSE's separate circular with the Muhurat trading timings for Diwali
  • Any change to the list announced by the exchange
