# TotalMarket — Free-only logo sourcing policy

Budget: **₹0**. Do not use paid logo APIs, subscriptions, trials requiring payment, or per-request charges.

Source priority:
1. Official company media kits where usage terms permit the intended display/rehosting.
2. Wikimedia Commons files with reviewed file-description page, licensing and trademark notices.
3. Other genuinely free repositories **only** where individual asset rights and authenticity are verified.

Do not treat “free download” as permission to redistribute. Keep official trademarks unchanged: original bytes, dimensions, aspect ratio, format and colors. Do not convert to 256×256 or crop rectangular marks.

For every logo record: legal company name, symbol, ISIN where applicable, original asset URL, file-description/rights URL, source site, license or permission basis, reviewer decision, download date and original SHA-256. Keep files out of the published logo registry until rights and identity are confirmed.

Use a neutral text fallback when no suitable asset is available. Do not hotlink a provider API without checking terms, reliability, attribution and caching policies.

No payment or publication without explicit approval.
