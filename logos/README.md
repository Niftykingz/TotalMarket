# Company logos

Upload logo files here named by NSE/US ticker in lowercase, for example:

- `infy.png` (Infosys)
- `reliance.png` (Reliance Industries)
- `jio.png` (Jio Platforms)
- `nvda.png` (Nvidia)

PNG with a transparent background works best (square or wide, at least 200 px tall). JPG also works. 
The site then shows the logo next to that company's ticker on story pages and on cover images.
Only upload logos you are allowed to use (for example from a company's official media/press kit).
