# Original TotalMarket graphics — review checklist

Design and own all article infographics, charts, layouts, data labels and editorial illustrations. No competitor screenshots, tracing, re-rendering copyrighted artwork, or copied stock photography.

The reusable preview lives at `graphics/preview.html`, and the 15-company logo review board at `graphics/logo-gallery.html`. All letter tiles are placeholders, not logos.

For charts: only verified market data with source, timestamp, market and units. For conceptual images: label illustrative content where it might be confused with real events. For official company logos: retain original bytes, aspect ratio, colors, and format, with documented usage rights. Keep rights-unclear logos out of the production registry.

Suggested next original template families: market wrap, earnings comparison, FII/DII flows, IPO timeline, policy rate, inflation, sector heatmap, gold/silver, forex movement, global indices, tech launch, education.
