# TotalMarket visual sourcing policy

## Official company logos
Use original, unmodified company logos only when source and applicable use permissions are documented. Prefer official company press/brand kits and published media-usage terms. Company logos are trademarks; a public URL does not itself grant permission. Never use search thumbnails, competitor watermarks, or generated imitations as official logos. Preserve proportions and transparent background. Store original source URL, access date, usage notes, and review decision. If rights are unclear, leave logo unapproved and use a neutral initial-based placeholder.

## Original graphics
Generate TotalMarket-owned charts, data infographics, editorial layouts and conceptual illustrations from independently verified factual inputs. Do not copy competitor compositions, screenshots, illustrations, watermarks or licensed stock photos. Use properly licensed fonts and icons. Mark illustrative visuals as illustrative where readers could mistake them for real photographs or live market charts.

## Publication controls
An editor must approve all new graphics before publishing. Verify company identity, article association, data source, period, units, timestamps, and image rights. No automated publishing, spending, or deployment without approval.
